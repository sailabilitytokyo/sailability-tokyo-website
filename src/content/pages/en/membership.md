Sailability Tokyo promotes sailing so that everyone can enjoy the water, regardless of age, disability or experience.

We hold regular events in Tokyo. We are looking for members who share our goals and can help with our activities.

To learn more about what we do, see [About Sailability Tokyo](/en/about).

## Individual and family members

For individuals and families who support our activities.

### Benefits

- You can join our sailing practice sessions

### Fees

- Joining fee: 1,000 yen
- Annual fee: 5,000 yen
- Family members: 1,000 yen a year for each additional person (no joining fee)

## Corporate members

For companies and organizations that support our activities.

### Benefits

- Your members (such as employees) can sail in the same way as individual members
- We put your logo on a Hansa and introduce you to the local community
- We show your logo on our website

### Fees

- Joining fee: 10,000 yen
- Annual fee: 20,000 yen

## How to apply

### Step 1　Send us an email

Please email us with the following details. Japanese or English is fine.
To: [sailabilitytokyo@gmail.com](mailto:sailabilitytokyo@gmail.com)

- Email address
- Type of membership
- Name
- Phone number
- Postal code
- Address
- Gender
- Date of birth

### Step 2　Pay the fees

Please transfer the joining fee and the annual fee to our bank account. Your membership runs for one year from the day we receive your payment.

### Unpaid fees

If fees are unpaid for two years or more, we will ask for your wishes and then proceed with withdrawal or a break in your membership.

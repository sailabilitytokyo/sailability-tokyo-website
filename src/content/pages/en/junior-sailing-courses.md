Not in elementary school? Anyone can try sailing at our sailing experience sessions. Come with your family, or even your pet.
[About the sailing experience](/en/sailing-experience)

## Feel the wind and learn to think for yourself

Sailing builds judgment, the courage to try, and teamwork. These are hard to learn from games or study alone.

A sailboat has no engine. You watch the wind and your surroundings, catch the wind and move forward. It is a series of thinking, trying and overcoming on your own.

While having fun, children grow in confidence and in their ability to think.

## Safe, even for beginners

- We use boats that do not capsize, developed so that people with disabilities can sail too
- The boats are safe enough for wheelchair users and for people with visual or hearing impairments
- Experienced staff support the children
- Children also learn about safety on the water and what to do if they fall in

Koto City has a lot of waterfront. Learning **how to protect yourself in an emergency** is an important lesson for children.

## Skills that sailing builds

Children build life skills that are hard to learn at school, while having fun.

- **Judgment**: watching the wind and other boats to decide where to go
- **Problem solving**: finding a way through when things do not go as planned
- **Communication**: working together with friends to take on a challenge

When the boat finally goes where they want, children feel a great sense of achievement and confidence.

## For children who live near the water

Koto City has many rivers and canals. Because the water is so close, it is important to learn how to enjoy it safely.

Knowing the waterfront around you and how to enjoy it safely will help in daily life, too.

Children from outside Koto City are also welcome. The pier is a 10-minute walk from Toyosu Station (Yurakucho Line and Yurikamome).

## Beginners are very welcome

"My child is not very sporty. Is that OK?" "We have never sailed before. Can my child do it?"
Children like this can join with confidence.

Sailing is a sport that uses both body and mind. It suits children who love playing outside and children who like to think things through.

Almost all the children start as complete beginners.

### Advanced course for returning children

For children joining for the second time or more, we offer an advanced course to build their skills further.
